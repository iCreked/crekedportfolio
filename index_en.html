ABOUT ME

Hi! I'm Creked, a professional video editor and sound designer. My primary goal is to create compelling visual content that hooks the audience and drives viewer retention from the first second to the very last frame.

I specialize in high-converting short-form content like Reels, Shorts, and TikToks featuring modern typography and motion graphics. I also handle end-to-end post-production for YouTube shows, podcasts, and commercial brand videos. Every project I take on receives deep attention to sound design (SFX), professional color grading, and precise pacing.

My workflow is built around industry-standard tools: Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, and DaVinci Resolve. Whether you need slick editing for short videos or complex multi-cam editing for large-scale projects, I am ready to deliver top-tier results.
