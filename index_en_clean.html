ABOUT ME

Hi ! My name is Alexander. and I’m a video editor. My main goal is to create content that keeps viewers watching right up to the very last second.

I specialize in creating dynamic gaming videos, Reels, Shorts, and TikToks featuring trending infographics, as well as comprehensive editing for YouTube shows, podcasts, and commercials. I bring meticulous attention to sound design, color grading, and precise narrative pacing to every project.

I work with Adobe Premiere Pro and Adobe After Effects. I am equipped to handle everything from the rapid styling of short-form clips to complex multi-camera editing for large-scale projects.
